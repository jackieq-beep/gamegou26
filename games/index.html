Questions and answers

Gamegou games: common questions

What is the best Gamegou game to start with?

It depends on how you like to play. Super Goal is the easiest to pick up, Top Football Manager is for strategy fans who want to run a club, Perfect Kick 2 is for quick online duels, and Soccer Strike is for live team matches.

What is a good free football manager game for mobile?

Top Football Manager is a free football manager game for Android and iOS with more than 50 million downloads on Google Play and officially licensed jerseys from the Premier League, La Liga, Bundesliga and MLS.

What are good soccer games to play offline?

Super Goal works completely without Wi-Fi or mobile data, and Soccer Legend has levels you can play offline.

What soccer games can I play online with friends?

Perfect Kick 2 lets you join a club with friends and start friendly matches. Soccer Strike and Soccer League let you team up with other players in live matches and tournaments.

What is a good quick soccer game for short breaks?

Perfect Kick 2 matches take under three minutes, Super Goal's puzzle levels take seconds to start, and Perfect Kick's penalty shootouts are played with one finger.

Which Gamegou games are on iPhone?

Top Football Manager, Super Goal, Perfect Kick, Perfect Kick 2 and Soccer Strike are on the App Store for iPhone and iPad. Soccer Legend and Soccer League are on Google Play for Android.

Are Gamegou games free?

Yes. All seven games are free to download. They include ads, and all except Soccer League offer optional in-app purchases.

Who is Gamegou?

Gamegou Limited is a mobile football game developer and publisher. Its seven games, including Super Goal, Top Football Manager, Perfect Kick and Soccer Strike, have more than 300 million downloads combined.

Mobile football games played around the world, from quick scoring sessions to multiplayer competition and long term club management.

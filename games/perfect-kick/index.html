Questions and answers

Perfect Kick questions

What is Perfect Kick?

Perfect Kick is a penalty shootout soccer game. You take and save penalties with a single finger, playing against opponents from around the world.

Is Perfect Kick a good penalty game?

Perfect Kick was named in Google Play's Best of 2015 and has more than 10 million downloads. It's rated 4.4 out of 5 from more than 680,000 reviews on Google Play (as of October 2026).

Is Perfect Kick free?

Yes. It's free to download on Google Play and the App Store. It includes ads and optional in-app purchases.

Is Perfect Kick suitable for kids?

Yes. Google Play rates it Everyone and the App Store rates it 4+.

What's the difference between Perfect Kick and Perfect Kick 2?

Perfect Kick is the original one-finger penalty shootout, first released in 2013. Perfect Kick 2 is the sequel from 2020, with fast 1v1 free-kick duels, power-ups, leagues and clubs.

What game modes does Perfect Kick have?

Champion League, Super Star Challenge and Classic Tournament, with global rankings.

What languages is Perfect Kick available in?

Perfect Kick is available in 15 languages, including English, Spanish, Portuguese, French, German, Arabic, Hindi, Japanese and Chinese.

Who makes Perfect Kick?

Perfect Kick is made by Gamegou Limited, a mobile football game developer and publisher whose games have more than 300 million downloads combined.

Questions and answers

Soccer League questions

What kind of game is Soccer League?

Soccer League is a real-time multiplayer soccer game. You team up with players from around the world in live matches, join competitive tournaments and play for both clubs and national teams.

Is Soccer League free?

Yes. Soccer League is free to download on Google Play and is supported by ads. It has no in-app purchases.

Is Soccer League on iPhone?

Not at the moment. Soccer League is available on Google Play for Android phones and tablets.

Do I need an internet connection to play Soccer League?

Yes. Matches are played live with and against real players, so you need to be online.

Can I play for a national team in Soccer League?

Yes. You can compete with both clubs and national teams.

Is Soccer League suitable for kids?

Google Play rates Soccer League Everyone.

Who makes Soccer League?

Soccer League is made by Gamegou Limited, a mobile football game developer and publisher whose games have more than 300 million downloads combined.

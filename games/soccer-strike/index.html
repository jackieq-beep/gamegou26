Questions and answers

Soccer Strike questions

What is Soccer Strike?

Soccer Strike is a real-time multiplayer soccer game. You play live matches against real opponents, team up in clubs and climb the global rankings.

Can I play Soccer Strike with friends?

Yes. You can join a club and help teammates with boosts, and compete together in tournaments.

Can I play for my country in Soccer Strike?

Yes. There are tournaments for both clubs and national teams.

Is Soccer Strike free?

Yes. It's free to download on Google Play and the App Store. It includes ads and optional in-app purchases.

Do I need an internet connection to play Soccer Strike?

Yes. Matches are played live against real players, so you need to be online.

Is Soccer Strike suitable for kids?

Google Play rates it Everyone. The App Store rates it 17+ because of simulated gambling content.

Can I play Soccer Strike on iPhone and iPad?

Yes. It runs on iPhone and iPad with iOS 15 or later, and on Android phones and tablets through Google Play.

Who makes Soccer Strike?

Soccer Strike is made by Gamegou Limited, a mobile football game developer and publisher whose games have more than 300 million downloads combined.

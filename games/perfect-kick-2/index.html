About Perfect Kick 2

Come play this fun free kick game against players worldwide in just under three minutes. Upgrade your stadium, unlock unique gear and power ups, and make your name in the Star Hall of Fame.

Perfect Kick 2 is the next evolution of Gamegou's beloved penalty series. Faster, wilder, and more feature packed than ever. Every match is a quick fire 1v1 duel that alternates between kicker and goalkeeper.

Fast Paced 1v1

Matches complete in under 3 minutes. Attack then defend. Every second matters.

Wild Power Ups

Tornado, Banana Kick, Runner and more. Unleash them at the perfect moment.

Leagues and Clubs

Compete in global leagues, join clubs with friends, and climb division rankings.

Wacky Costumes

Choose from an array of outrageous outfits and gear to stand out on the pitch.

Questions and answers

Perfect Kick 2 questions

What is Perfect Kick 2?

Perfect Kick 2 is an online soccer game where you face real players in quick 1v1 free-kick duels, switching between kicker and goalkeeper.

How long is a Perfect Kick 2 match?

Under three minutes, which makes it a good soccer game for short breaks.

Can I play Perfect Kick 2 with friends?

Yes. You can join a club with friends, chat with other players and start friendly matches.

Is Perfect Kick 2 free?

Yes. It's free to download on Google Play and the App Store. It includes ads and optional in-app purchases.

Is Perfect Kick 2 suitable for kids?

Google Play rates it Everyone and the App Store rates it 4+. The App Store notes that it contains loot boxes.

How is Perfect Kick 2 rated?

It's rated 4.7 out of 5 from nearly 20,000 reviews on the App Store and 4.4 from about 170,000 reviews on Google Play (as of October 2026).

What power-ups are in Perfect Kick 2?

Power-ups include Tornado, Banana Kick and Runner, among others.

Who makes Perfect Kick 2?

Perfect Kick 2 is made by Gamegou Limited, a mobile football game developer and publisher whose games have more than 300 million downloads combined.

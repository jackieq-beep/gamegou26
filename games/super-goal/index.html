About Super Goal

Draw a line to kick the ball. The direction and route of the ball are completely controlled by you. Use your wisdom to solve puzzles and score top notch goals!

Super Goal has more than 50 million downloads worldwide. It revolutionizes mobile soccer with its unique draw to kick mechanic. No complicated buttons, no confusing joysticks. Just pure, creative soccer action.

Draw to Kick

Simply draw a line on screen to map out the perfect trajectory for your shot.

Diverse Locations

Wild Ball Selection

Football, basketball, tennis, volleyball, golf and even fruit and animal balls!

Fully Offline

No internet required. Perfect for commutes, travel, or anywhere you want to play.

Questions and answers

Super Goal questions

How do you play Super Goal?

You draw a line on the screen and the ball follows it. Each level is a small soccer puzzle: find a path around the defenders and into the goal.

Can I play Super Goal offline?

Yes. Super Goal works without Wi-Fi or mobile data, so it's a good soccer game for planes, trains and anywhere without signal.

Is Super Goal free?

Yes. Super Goal is free to download on Google Play and the App Store. It includes ads and optional in-app purchases.

Is Super Goal suitable for kids?

Google Play rates it Everyone. The App Store rates it 12+ because of mild cartoon violence and mild simulated gambling.

How popular is Super Goal?

Super Goal has more than 50 million downloads. It's rated 4.7 out of 5 from about 22,000 reviews on the App Store and 4.5 from nearly 200,000 reviews on Google Play (as of October 2026).

What devices and languages does Super Goal support?

It runs on Android phones and tablets, and on iPhone and iPad with iOS 15 or later. It's available in 18 languages, including English, Spanish, Portuguese, French, German, Arabic, Japanese and Chinese.

What makes Super Goal different from other soccer games?

Instead of buttons or joysticks, you draw each shot. Levels take place on beaches, city rooftops, cruise ships and snow arenas, and you can even kick basketballs, tennis balls, fruit and animal balls.

Who makes Super Goal?

Super Goal is made by Gamegou Limited, a mobile football game developer and publisher whose games have more than 300 million downloads combined.

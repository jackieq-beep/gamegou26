About Top Football Manager

Do you dream of coaching a team of eleven soccer stars hungry to be champions? Top Football Manager puts sports fans like you in charge! Bid on and scout for premier players, build and train a team with special skills, test tactics and formations against real opponents from around the world, and watch the match in live 3D simulation.

With officially licensed jerseys from Premier League, La Liga, Bundesliga, and MLS, this is the most authentic football management experience on mobile.

Scout and Sign Players

Hunt for stars in the transfer market. Bid, recruit agents, or find young hopefuls with scouts.

Tactics and Formations

Set mentality, attack direction, defensive line. React in real time to opponents.

Develop Your Club

Build stadium, youth academy, hospital, utilities, and training ground facilities.

Manage the National Team

Take charge of your nation and lead them to international glory.

Questions and answers

Top Football Manager questions

Is Top Football Manager a good football manager game for mobile?

Top Football Manager has more than 50 million downloads on Google Play. You scout and sign players, set tactics, build your club's facilities and watch every match in live 3D.

Does Top Football Manager have real club kits?

Yes. It features officially licensed jerseys from the Premier League, La Liga, Bundesliga and MLS.

Is Top Football Manager free to play?

Yes. It's free to download on Google Play and the App Store. It includes ads and optional in-app purchases.

Can I play Top Football Manager on iPhone and iPad?

Yes. It runs on iPhone and iPad with iOS 12 or later, and on Android phones and tablets through Google Play.

What languages is Top Football Manager available in?

Top Football Manager is available in 20 languages, including English, Spanish, Portuguese, French, German, Arabic, Turkish, Japanese and Chinese.

Is Top Football Manager suitable for kids?

Google Play rates it Everyone. The App Store rates it 12+ because of frequent competitive contests and mild simulated gambling.

How is Top Football Manager rated?

It's rated 4.5 out of 5 on the App Store and 3.8 out of 5 from more than 430,000 reviews on Google Play (as of October 2026).

When did Top Football Manager come out?

Top Football Manager first came out in 2017 and is still updated regularly, including the current Top Football Manager 2026.

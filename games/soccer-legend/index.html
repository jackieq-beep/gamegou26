Show your soccer skills, shape your strategy, and master the perfect fusion of soccer and puzzle play. Outsmart rivals, strike incredible goals, and rise from the streets to the world stage.

Questions and answers

Soccer Legend questions

What kind of game is Soccer Legend?

Soccer Legend is a football puzzle game. Each level is a match situation you solve with smart passes, shots and super skills as you rise from street football to the world stage.

Can I play Soccer Legend offline?

Yes. Soccer Legend has levels you can play without Wi-Fi, plus online multiplayer when you're connected.

Is Soccer Legend free?

Yes. It's free to download on Google Play. It includes ads and optional in-app purchases.

Is Soccer Legend on iPhone?

Not at the moment. Soccer Legend is available on Google Play for Android phones and tablets.

How is Soccer Legend rated?

Soccer Legend is rated 4.7 out of 5 from about 67,000 reviews on Google Play (as of October 2026).

Is Soccer Legend suitable for kids?

Google Play rates Soccer Legend Everyone.

Can I customise my player in Soccer Legend?

Yes. You can change your star's hairstyle, kit, boots and more.

Who makes Soccer Legend?

Soccer Legend is made by Gamegou Limited, a mobile football game developer and publisher whose games have more than 300 million downloads combined.
